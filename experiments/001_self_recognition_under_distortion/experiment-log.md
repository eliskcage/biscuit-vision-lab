## Objective

# Major Findings

## Finding 001 — Failure Reveals Observer Bias

### Observation
The radial eye locked strongly onto circles but struggled with triangles and squares.

### Interpretation
The observer itself encoded circular assumptions.

### Architectural Discovery
Different shape families require different observer species.

```text
circle eye != triangle eye != square eye
```
